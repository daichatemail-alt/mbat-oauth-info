MBAT Gmail Read Only

A personal Windows desktop tool for reviewing promotional emails forwarded to a dedicated Gmail account. This information site does not provide access to the tool or connect visitors to Gmail.

The tool reads selected forwarded promotional emails from Ladbrokes, 7bet and kwiff, records their source and dates, and extracts offer terms for manual review. Missing eligibility or date evidence and expired offers block further qualification.

Google access is limited to Gmail read-only permission. The application enforces the dedicated account dai.mbat.leads@gmail.com. It does not send, edit or delete email, open offer links or attachments, connect to bookmakers or exchanges, or place bets.

This is a personal tool, not a public service. Google OAuth production status does not mean MBAT software has been released or deployed.
